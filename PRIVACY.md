## What this keyboard does NOT do

- It does not collect, log, or transmit anything you type to any server
  operated by the developer.
- It does not show ads or contain analytics/tracking SDKs.
- It does not require an account or sign-in.

## Permissions it uses, and why

| Permission | Why | Where the data goes |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | Voice typing | Handled entirely by Android's built-in speech recognition service (provided by your device/Google Play Services). Audio is not sent to, or stored by, this app or its developer. |
| Internet (`INTERNET`) | GIF search | Only the text you type while the GIF search panel is open is sent to Tenor's search API, using the API key you (or the app's publisher) provided. No other network requests are made. |
| Vibration (`VIBRATE`) | Optional key-press haptic feedback | Local only, not data-related. |

## Clipboard history

If enabled (on by default, toggle in Settings), copied text is stored
**only on your device**, in the app's private storage. It is never
uploaded anywhere. You can clear individual items from the clipboard panel.

## Custom fonts & background images

Font files and background images you import are copied into the app's
private storage on your device only, so they keep working even if you
remove the original file. They are never uploaded anywhere.

## Data shared with third parties

- **Tenor (Google)**: search queries you type while GIF search is open,
  and the API key used for the request. Subject to
  [Tenor's own privacy policy](https://tenor.com/legal-privacy). Nothing
  is sent unless you actively open GIF search and type something.
- **Android's speech recognition service**: audio captured while you're
  actively using voice typing, handled per Android's own privacy terms
  for that service. Nothing is captured unless you tap the mic icon.

No other third parties receive any data from this app.
